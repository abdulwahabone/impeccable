## Torn Paper shader

The WebGL shader in `pro/src/scripts/torn-paper-worlds.js`, which backs the impeccable.pro waitlist page, is adapted from the "Torn Paper" study in the `radiant` shader collection. The tear geometry, paper surface, and plasma light field are from the original; the reveal was rewritten to sample a world card from the Impeccable catalog, and reduced motion holds the cycle open instead of freezing it shut.

**Original work:** https://github.com/pbakaus/radiant (https://radiant-shaders.com/shader/torn-paper)
**Original license:** MIT
**Author:** Paul Bakaus
